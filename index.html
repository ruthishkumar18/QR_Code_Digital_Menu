The slow cup philosophy

Moss & Ember began with one idea: coffee tastes better when nobody is hurrying it. We roast in small batches, rest the beans for a week, and pull every shot to the clock and to the nose.

Our milk is steamed until it sounds like rain, our teas steep in cast-iron pots, and our kitchen bakes with jaggery, cardamom and whatever the market had that morning.

Sit longer. Sip slower.
